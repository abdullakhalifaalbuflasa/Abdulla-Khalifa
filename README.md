Abdullah Khalifa’s Bahraini Literature and Its Luminary Figures surveys the history of Bahraini literature and highlights its most prominent literary figures through a profound cultural and intellectual journey.
Book Overview:
In this work, Abdullah Khalifa takes the reader deep into the roots of Bahraini society, tracing the development of local literature through the early cultural clubs. Within these circles, creative young writers confronted a state of “social dormancy” through poetry and fiction, transforming pain and despair into a creative force that transcended the islands’ geographical boundaries and addressed humanity at large. The book is not confined to historical documentation; rather, it offers an aesthetic and intellectual journey that connects past and present and reveals how intellectuals confronted the struggles of colonialism and underdevelopment through the power of critical consciousness.
Luminary Literary Figures:

Ahmed Al-Shamlan

Amin Saleh
These figures are portrayed as meteors illuminating the literary firmament, transforming difficult experiences into works of art imbued with humanistic messages.
The Development of the Short Story and the Novel
The book traces the development of the “unfettered short story” and the emergence of the novel as an instrument for constructing national identity. It elucidates literature’s role in interpreting the features of popular society and analysing the dynamics of change and renaissance in Bahrain. It also highlights the creative capacity of young writers to confront social and political challenges.
Significance of the Book
Bahraini Literature and Its Luminary Figures constitutes a cultural and aesthetic legacy, as well as a tribute to those who devoted their time and effort to enriching Bahraini literature. It offers a comprehensive account of how suffering may be transformed into creativity and how individual experience may be connected to the broader Bahraini society.
Bahraini Literature and Its Luminary Figures, by the late Bahraini writer and novelist Abdullah Khalifa Al-Buflasa (1948–2026), is an important documentary and critical study that chronicles the trajectory of Bahraini literature—including the short story, the novel, and poetry—and relates it to social and political transformations. The book foregrounds the struggle of the Bahraini individual against poverty and the constraints of inherited tradition while highlighting the works of major literary figures.

Abdullah Khalifa and His Symbolic Significance:
Abdullah Khalifa (Al-Buflasa) was himself a prominent writer and critic. Active during the 1960s, he was subjected to imprisonment and centred his writings on national concerns, social transformations, the lives of seafarers, and artistic practice as a form of national responsibility.
The book may be regarded as a “compass” for understanding Bahraini literature and its history from a critical and analytical perspective that connects literary expression to social and cultural “haemorrhage.”
